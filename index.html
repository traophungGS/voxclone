🎙️ How to Record Clean Phonemes

Click Record (or press & hold), pronounce the short phonetic sound clearly for ~0.4–0.7 seconds, then click Stop. Silence at the start and end is trimmed automatically! Even recording just the Vowels (A, E, I, O, U) lets the engine speak full sentences.

Custom Word & Phrase Dictionary

Record whole words (like your name, "hello", "awesome", or "world"). Whenever these words appear in your text, the synthesizer will use your exact whole-word recording instead of stitching phonemes!
